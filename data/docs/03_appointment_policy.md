# Appointment Policy

## How to book

You can book an appointment through this WhatsApp assistant, by calling reception, or at the front desk. To book, we need your full name, the service you want, and your preferred date and time. Appointments are confirmed instantly when a slot is available, and you receive a booking reference starting with "APT-".

Standard appointment slots are 30 minutes long.

## Rescheduling and cancellation

- You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment.
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment are charged a late-cancellation fee of ₹300.
- To cancel or reschedule, message us with your booking reference. A staff member confirms the change.

## Late arrival

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten your treatment or move you to another slot, depending on the day's schedule.

## No-shows

If you miss an appointment without telling us, a no-show fee of ₹500 is added to your next bill. After two no-shows, future bookings require a ₹1,000 advance deposit, which is adjusted against your treatment bill.

## What to bring

- A photo ID.
- Previous dental X-rays or reports, if you have them.
- A list of medicines you currently take.
- Your insurance card, if you are claiming insurance.

## Emergency appointments

We keep two emergency slots open every working day for severe toothache, broken teeth, or swelling. Emergency visits are charged ₹700 plus any treatment cost. If you have heavy bleeding that will not stop, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or swelling spreading to your eye or neck, go to the nearest hospital emergency department immediately — do not wait for a dental appointment.
