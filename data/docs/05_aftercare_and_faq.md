# Aftercare Guidance and Frequently Asked Questions

The guidance below is general information from the clinic. It is not a diagnosis. If symptoms are severe or getting worse, contact the clinic or seek emergency care.

## After a filling

Avoid chewing on the treated side until the numbness wears off, usually 2 to 3 hours. Mild sensitivity to cold for a few days is common. If your bite feels high after the numbness wears off, contact us for a quick adjustment at no charge.

## After a root canal treatment

Some tenderness for 2 to 3 days is normal. Avoid chewing hard food on that tooth until the permanent crown is fitted, because the tooth can crack. Take any medicines exactly as prescribed by your dentist.

## After teeth cleaning

Gums may feel slightly sore and teeth may be sensitive for a day or two. Avoid very hot or very cold drinks for 24 hours.

## After whitening

Avoid tea, coffee, red wine, turmeric-heavy food, and smoking for 48 hours. Temporary sensitivity is common and usually settles within 2 days.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you see children?
Yes. Dr. Neha Iyer sees children aged 2 to 14. Children must come with a parent or guardian.

### Is teeth cleaning painful?
Most patients feel only mild discomfort. If you have sensitive teeth, tell the dentist and they can use a numbing gel.

### Can I get treatment while pregnant?
Routine check-ups and cleanings are safe during pregnancy. Please tell the dentist you are pregnant before any X-ray or treatment so they can plan accordingly.

### How long do braces take?
Most braces treatments take 12 to 24 months. Dr. Kabir Mehta gives you a personalised estimate at the orthodontic consultation.

### Do you sterilise instruments?
Yes. All reusable instruments are sterilised in a Class B autoclave after every patient, and single-use items are never reused.

### Can I get a second opinion?
Yes. Book a new patient consultation and bring your previous reports and X-rays.

### Is there Wi-Fi in the waiting area?
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available for patients. Ask reception for the password.
