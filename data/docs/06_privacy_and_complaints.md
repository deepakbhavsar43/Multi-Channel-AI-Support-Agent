# Privacy, Complaints, and Support Requests

## Privacy

We keep patient records confidential and use them only for your treatment, billing, and appointment reminders. This WhatsApp assistant stores your messages so our staff can follow up on your requests. Messages are kept for 90 days and then deleted. You can ask us to delete your chat history at any time by raising a support request.

The assistant never asks for card numbers, CVV, OTPs, or passwords. Do not share them in chat.

## Medical records

You can request a copy of your dental records and X-rays. Copies are emailed within 3 working days after we verify your identity.

## Complaints and feedback

If you are unhappy with any part of your visit, tell us. Complaints are logged as a support ticket and reviewed by the clinic manager, who will contact you within 1 working day. Urgent clinical concerns are escalated to the treating dentist the same day.

## Support requests the assistant can raise

The assistant can create a support ticket for:

- Duplicate invoices or billing questions.
- Insurance pre-authorisation help.
- Requests for medical records.
- Complaints and feedback.
- Cancelling or rescheduling an existing appointment.
- Anything the assistant cannot answer from clinic information.

Each ticket gets a reference starting with "TKT-". Staff reply during working hours.

## Talking to a person

You can ask to speak to a person at any time, for example by typing "talk to a human" or "agent". During working hours a staff member usually replies within 15 minutes. Outside working hours, your request is queued and answered the next working morning.
