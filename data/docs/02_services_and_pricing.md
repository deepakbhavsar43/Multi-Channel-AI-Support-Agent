# Services and Pricing

All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR), include GST, and are indicative. The final cost is confirmed by the dentist after an examination.

## Consultation and check-up

- New patient consultation (exam + treatment plan): ₹500, about 30 minutes.
- Routine check-up for existing patients: ₹300, about 20 minutes.
- Digital X-ray (single): ₹250. Full-mouth OPG X-ray: ₹900.

The consultation fee is waived if you start a treatment worth more than ₹5,000 within 30 days.

## Cleaning and preventive care

- Scaling and polishing (teeth cleaning): ₹1,500, about 45 minutes.
- Fluoride treatment: ₹600.
- Pit and fissure sealants: ₹800 per tooth.

We recommend a cleaning every 6 months.

## Fillings and root canal treatment

- Tooth-coloured composite filling: ₹1,800 to ₹3,000 per tooth depending on size.
- Root canal treatment (RCT), front tooth: ₹5,500. Molar: ₹7,500 to ₹9,000.
- A crown after root canal treatment is charged separately (see Crowns).

Root canal treatment usually needs 2 visits.

## Crowns and bridges

- Metal-ceramic crown: ₹6,000 per tooth.
- Zirconia crown: ₹12,000 per tooth.

Crowns are made by our partner dental lab. The lab takes 5 to 7 working days. You can ask this assistant for the status of your lab order using your order ID.

## Orthodontics

- Orthodontic consultation with Dr. Kabir Mehta: ₹800.
- Metal braces (full treatment): from ₹35,000.
- Clear aligners (full treatment): from ₹1,20,000.

Aligner sets are manufactured by an external lab; delivery takes about 3 weeks after scanning.

## Teeth whitening

- In-clinic whitening (one session, about 60 minutes): ₹8,000.
- Take-home whitening kit with custom trays: ₹6,000.

## Children's dentistry

- Child check-up with Dr. Neha Iyer: ₹400.
- Children aged under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

## Services we do not offer

We do not offer dental implants, wisdom tooth surgery under general anaesthesia, or cosmetic veneers. We can refer you to a specialist for these.
