# Insurance and Payments

## Payment methods

We accept cash, UPI, debit cards, and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay). We do not accept cheques.

## EMI options

Treatments above ₹15,000 (for example braces, aligners, or multiple crowns) can be paid in no-cost EMI over 3 or 6 months on selected credit cards. EMI is not available for consultations or cleaning.

## Dental insurance

We are a cashless partner with the following demo insurers: SafeSmile Health (demo), CareShield Insurance (demo), and MediTrust General (demo). For other insurers we provide an itemised bill and treatment report so you can file a reimbursement claim yourself.

For cashless treatment, please share your policy number at least 2 working days before your appointment so we can get pre-authorisation. Most dental insurance plans do not cover cosmetic treatments such as whitening or aligners.

## Invoices and receipts

An invoice is sent to your registered email after every payment. If you need a duplicate invoice, ask this assistant to raise a request and our billing team will email it within 2 working days.

## Refunds

Advance deposits are refundable if you cancel more than 24 hours before the appointment. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Payments for completed treatment are not refundable, but if you are unhappy with a treatment please raise a complaint and the clinic manager will contact you.
